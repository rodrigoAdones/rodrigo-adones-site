# Homepage (`/`)

**Mode:** Persuade. Hiring decision-makers and practitioner readers equally; the four pillars are the map, the articles the proof. No intro bio this pass; newsletter form stubbed (provider undecided).

**Anti-goals:** generic dev portfolio, startup landing page, playful gimmicks. No planes, radar, or cockpit costume: the ATC grammar carries only real post metadata.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is a flight progress board: every post is a paper strip racked in one of four pillar bays, and the board is read against one "now" rule. It refuses the name-plus-bio hero over a card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Grey-green enamel board (#D3DAD4), paper strips colour-coded per pillar (buff #EAD7A2 ai-delivery, blue #A7C4E2 platform-dx, salmon #EFB4A0 delivery-metrics, green #C6DB9C commerce-payments), aluminium holder rims, grease-pencil black #141414, one red pencil #C4261D for the now rule and the active state. B612 / B612 Mono self-hosted. Strips are ruled field grids; only the newest strip is solid-inked. Raises: manga (tonal economy), flyer (one element wins), daylight (now rule), ebru (permanent sequence numbers), brine (status field).

STORY: The visitor learns who (callsign), what four areas (route and bays), and that writing actually happens (dated strips), then opens a strip, a bay, or files their email.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left rail with the nav designators. Across the top of the main area, the red now rule with build time in UTC. Below it, "RODRIGO ADONES" as a callsign strip at poster scale, with the four pillars as its route field. Then the inked newest strip, then the latest rack beginning. The primary action is the inked strip's title link.

FORM: Flight progress strip board, #6 on the ordered list, seed 46ed9181. Signature interaction: a strip slides partway out of its holder on hover and focus (CSS only).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
